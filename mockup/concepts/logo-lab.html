Wingpath

The figure-eight a hummingbird’s wing traces as it hovers. The larger loop is the downstroke, which does most of the lifting.

Built from
Two circles and the two lines that touch both. One unbroken stroke that passes over itself once.
Tones
Ink only.
In motion
A point of light runs the path, then slows to a hover when you point at it.

Recommendation

The two we would take forward

Wingpath if the mark should tell the story. Shift if it should stay out of the way. Both are calm, both hold at 16 pixels, and both give the site one clear thing to animate.

Wingpath

  • It carries the whole story in one line: hover, the figure-eight, the downstroke.
  • The tilt, the uneven loops and the over-and-under crossing make it feel drawn, not typed.
  • It is built to move. The site’s signature moment can be a point of light running the path.
  • One tone. It needs no colour to work, so it survives every surface.

Shift

  • The calmest mark here. One circle, one cut, nothing extra.
  • The two halves hold the throat-colour idea: dark one way, bright the other.
  • It stays clear at 16 pixels and reads as one solid shape in a crowded tab bar.
  • Simple to animate well: the halves slide into line once, then hold still.
