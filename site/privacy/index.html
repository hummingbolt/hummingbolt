Privacy

This website collects almost nothing: no cookies, no analytics, no forms.

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Who we are

This policy covers hummingbolt.com, run by Hummingbolt LLC, 2108 N St #18140, Sacramento, CA 95816. You can reach us at admin@hummingbolt.com.

This website

  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not have forms, accounts or sign-ups.
  • We do not load fonts, scripts or other content from third-party sites.

Information collected

Personal information can be collected in only two ways.

  • When you visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, and may also record technical details such as your browser type and the page you asked for. GitHub handles this data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs or use them to identify or track you.
  • When you email us. We receive your email address, your name if you include it, your message and anything you attach. Our email is hosted by Google Workspace, which stores messages for us. We use this information only to reply to you.

How we use it

We don’t sell your email or use it for marketing. No other company collects information about your activity across sites through this website.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep emails as long as we need them to help you, then delete them. Email admin@hummingbolt.com to see or delete what we hold.

Children

This site is not directed to children. If a child under 13 emails us, we reply once, if needed, and then delete the message.

Do Not Track

We don’t track visitors over time or across other sites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off.

Our apps and games

This policy covers only this website. Each of our products has its own privacy policy. For Toppleton, see the privacy policy linked from inside the game at play.toppleton.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: admin@hummingbolt.com.
